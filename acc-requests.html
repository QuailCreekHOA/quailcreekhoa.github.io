Architectural Control Committee (ACC) Requests

Per the Quail Creek CC&Rs, section 5.2, homeowners must obtain approval from the HOA board (the Architectural Control Committee) for most projects and clearings on homeowner property.

In addition, approval is required for signs erected in the Quail Creek front entrance. See the CC&Rs section 5.5 and section 5.9.

Examples of projects that require approval per the CCRs include the following:

  • Any sign to be displayed at the front entrance
  • House exterior painting
  • Fences or walls
  • Tree removal
  • Roof changes
  • Mass landscaping projects or redesign
  • Any additions or architectural changes to the exterior of the house
  • Sheds or other free standing structures such as playgrounds and dog runs
  • Hot tubs and spas
  • Air condition units or other equipment visible from the street
  • Any project which may involve the knowledge of or compliance with King County Codes

This is not a comprehensive list. In general, your best and easiest first step is to just send an email to qchoaboard@outlook.com with a brief project description, and we can walk you through the process, or whether approval is even necessary. The ACC may solicit feedback from neighbors, so we also recommend that you speak with your neighbors if your project may impact them.

It is the responsibility of the homeowner to obtain the necessary information and/or approval from the county for any project that must comply with King County Codes.

All applications and county approval must be submitted in writing to the ACC for approval to the board mailing address or email at qchoaboard@outlook.com.

Any other questions? Email us at qchoaboard@outlook.com.
